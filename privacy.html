Some games may store information such as high scores directly in your browser using local storage. This allows your browser to remember certain game information between visits. This information may be removed by clearing your browser's stored site data.

Keldiq Games may use third-party services for website hosting, analytics, security, advertising, or other website functionality. Those services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

Keldiq Games does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The website currently allows games to be played without creating an account or submitting personal information.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Keldiq Games adds new features, services, accounts, advertising, or other functionality. The updated date shown at the top of this page will indicate when changes were made.

Contact
